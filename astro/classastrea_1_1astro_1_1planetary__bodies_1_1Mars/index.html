function find_atmospheric_density¶

Calculate the atmospheric density at a given altitude and date. 

virtual Density astrea::astro::planetary_bodies::Mars::find_atmospheric_density (
    const  Date & date,
    const  Distance & altitude
) override const

This function uses a simple exponential model to estimate the atmospheric density based on the altitude above the surface of Mars. The model parameters are based on average conditions and do not account for seasonal or diurnal variations.

Parameters:

  • date The date for which to calculate the atmospheric density.
  • altitude The altitude above the surface of Mars in kilometers.

Returns:

Density The estimated atmospheric density in kg/m^3.
